Operations research between theory and practice

Optimisation models and solution methods for production, supply chains and asset management. Industrial problems raise new questions for the theory, and the methods developed to answer them return to practice as decision-support systems.

Three lines of research. Each moves between a class of industrial problems and its theory.

Production planning and scheduling

Lot-sizing and scheduling formulations and hybrid solution methods for process industries such as beverages, glass and pulp and paper. Planning cases with Super Bock motivated the models and served to validate them at industrial scale.

Key papers

  • EJOR 2013 Pricing, relaxing and fixing under lot sizing and scheduling
  • Omega 2017 Tactical production and distribution planning with dependency issues on the production process

Integrated production, inventory and routing

New variants of production-routing and vehicle-routing problems, with delivery time windows and product-dependent deliveries, formulated from food retail and distribution and solved at the size of real networks.

Key papers

  • Omega 2019 Solving a large multi-product production-routing problem with delivery time windows
  • Transportation Research Part E 2018 The time window assignment vehicle routing problem with product dependent deliveries

Asset management under degradation

Models of asset condition and degradation, and the maintenance and investment decisions that follow from them, for portfolios of power transformers, hydropower and wind assets, developed with E-Redes, EDP and three European projects.

Key papers

  • Reliability Engineering & System Safety 2021 Resource definition and allocation for a multi-asset portfolio with heterogeneous degradation

European projects

XFLEX · EU-SCORES · InteGrid

Theory and practice

The research runs in both directions: practice raises problems the literature had not yet formulated, and the models built to solve them are published and taken back to practice, where their limits raise the next question.

From theory to practice, and back

Transfer is part of the research cycle: it tests the methods at real scale and brings back the questions that the next projects study.
