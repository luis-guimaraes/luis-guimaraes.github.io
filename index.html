Three lines of research. Each grew out of projects with industry.

Plans production in process industries

Lot-sizing and scheduling models for beverages, glass and pulp and paper, from the long-term planning system built with Super Bock to dispatching rules learned from data.

Key papers

  • EJOR 2013 Pricing, relaxing and fixing under lot sizing and scheduling
  • Omega 2017 Tactical production and distribution planning with dependency issues on the production process

Routes deliveries and stock across supply chains

Production-routing and inventory-routing problems for food retail and distribution, solved at the size of a real network.

Key papers

  • Omega 2019 Solving a large multi-product production-routing problem with delivery time windows
  • Transportation Research Part E 2018 The time window assignment vehicle routing problem with product dependent deliveries

Schedules maintenance of energy assets

Health indices, maintenance policies and budget allocation for power transformers, hydropower and wind, with E-Redes, EDP and three European projects.

Key papers

  • Reliability Engineering & System Safety 2021 Resource definition and allocation for a multi-asset portfolio with heterogeneous degradation

European projects

XFLEX · EU-SCORES · InteGrid

Research with industry

Of 37 research projects, 21 were funded by companies, and the planning systems built in them are used by planners in beverages, retail and energy.

From models to daily use

Research that leaves the university as a book, a company and systems that planners run.
